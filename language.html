Profile

Don Hartig is a certified and accredited TEFL English teacher and ranked translator in seven languages. Over a fifteen-year stay working as an English teacher in China, he developed an extensive mnemonic teaching system and taught learners of every age, from young children to working professionals. He is equally comfortable teaching one-on-one or in group classes, and can deliver deep-immersion, English-only instruction or multilingual instruction, whichever the learner requires.

Teaching & Lecturing in China

Don's fifteen years in China form the core of his teaching career, spanning both classroom instruction and university-level lecturing. He has held teaching and lecturing positions at prestigious institutions including Sichuan Foreign Language University and Chongqing University, where he worked alongside faculty and students across a wide range of academic disciplines and proficiency levels.

This extensive, sustained immersion in Chinese academic and educational life gave Don a rare vantage point: the ability to design instruction that speaks directly to the needs, habits, and learning culture of Chinese-speaking students, while remaining fully adaptable to learners from any linguistic background.

Language History, Philology & Applied Linguistics

Don's teaching is grounded in more than method — it draws on a deep, ongoing study of language history, philology, and applied linguistics. This background allows him to explain not just how a language works, but why it works that way: the historical shifts, etymological roots, and structural patterns that make grammar and vocabulary memorable rather than arbitrary. It is this insight, combined with genuine enthusiasm, that lets Don consistently make language learning feel engaging rather than rote.
