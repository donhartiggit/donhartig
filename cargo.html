How Verification Works

Every Trade Provably Correct,
Step by Step

  • 1

    Strategy Definition

    Client or vault manager defines and submits a strategy specification. The spec is stored immutably on-chain — it cannot be changed after the fact.

  • 2

    Execution + Quorm Oracle

    Sorites executes trades using a tamper-proof oracle price feed. All transactions are recorded on blockchain as an immutable event stream.

  • 3

    Quorm Proof Generation

    Quorm's off-chain proof system verifies: Did actions match the strategy? Were oracle prices used correctly? Were risk limits respected? Generates cryptographic pass/fail proof.

  • 4

    Attestation & Public Dashboard

    Proof, strategy hash, yield, and timestamp are written to the on-chain Attestation Registry. Any investor, auditor, or government body can verify at any time — no permission needed.
