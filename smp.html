The Treatment

Scalp Micropigmentation — The Science of Invisible

Scalp micropigmentation (SMP) is a highly specialised, non-surgical cosmetic procedure in which ultra-fine deposits of medical-grade pigment are applied to the scalp using precision micro-needles. Each dot is individually placed to replicate the natural appearance of a hair follicle — creating the illusion of a full, closely-cropped head of hair.

Unlike hair transplants — which involve surgical extraction and reimplantation of actual follicles — SMP requires no incisions, no anaesthesia, and no recovery time. The result is immediate, natural, and permanent. It is equally effective for pattern hair loss, alopecia, scalp scars, and those simply seeking greater density and definition.

✦ The SMP Treatment Process
01
Scalp & Design Consultation
A thorough analysis of the scalp, existing hair pattern, skin tone and facial structure to design a hairline that is indistinguishable from nature.
02
Pigment Matching
Medical-grade pigments are custom-blended to precisely match the client's natural hair colour — accounting for the scalp undertone and intended density.
03
Micro-Needling Application
Thousands of individual pigment deposits are placed with a specialist SMP machine, replicating follicles at the precise depth, angle and spacing that matches the client's natural pattern.
04
Layering & Refinement
Across 2–4 sessions, layering builds density and dimension — creating depth and a three-dimensional appearance utterly indistinguishable from a freshly-shaved head.
