The Craft

What Scalp Micropigmentation Actually Is

A precision technique that deposits pigment into the scalp's upper dermis, replicating the appearance of thousands of individual, closely-shaven hair follicles. Dr Don Hartig is recognised as one of Thailand's leading practitioners of this technique.

Non-Surgical
No incisions, no grafts — a needle depositing pigment, nothing more invasive.
Zero Downtime
Return to work and daily life immediately after every session.
Undetectable Result
Designed to be indistinguishable from a genuinely shaved hairline, even up close.
4–6 Year Longevity
A long-lasting result with simple, infrequent touch-up sessions.
